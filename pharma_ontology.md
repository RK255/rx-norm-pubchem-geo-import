# Pharma Knowledge Graph Ontology

## Focus Area 1: Chemical/Molecular Identity

- Chemical classification (ATC, UNII)
- Structure (2D/3D, formula, weight)
- BCS classification
- Biologics characterization

## Focus Area 2: Clinical Application

- **Indications** (ICD-mapped) — HIGH PRIORITY
- **Contraindications** — HIGH PRIORITY
- Dosage forms & routes
- Dosing (population-specific)
- Warnings/precautions
- Black box warnings
- Off-label uses

## Focus Area 3: Pharmacological Profile

- **Mechanism of action** / MeSH classifications — HIGH PRIORITY
- **ATC classifications** — HIGH PRIORITY
- **Drug-drug interactions (DDI)** — HIGH PRIORITY
- Pharmacokinetics (PK)
- Pharmacogenomics
- Adverse effect potential
- Target/receptor mapping

## Focus Area 4: Regulatory/Commercial Status

- Approval status (NDA/ANDA/BLA)
- **Biosimilar mappings** — HIGH PRIORITY
- Therapeutic equivalence
- Patent/exclusivity
- Pricing (NADAC, WAC, ASP)
- Controlled substance schedule
- Market status

## Focus Area 5: Evidence Foundation

- Clinical trials (NCT mapping)
- Real-world evidence (FAERS)
- Clinical guidelines
- Safety signals

## Focus Area 6: Supply Chain

- **Manufacturers/labelers** — HIGH PRIORITY
- Distribution channels
- Availability/shortages
- Product tracking

## Focus Area 7: Global (Future)

- International identifiers
- Regulatory pathways
- Reference pricing

---

## Priority Summary

1. **MoA + ATC classifications** — Core pharmacological understanding
2. **Manufacturer data** — Critical for supply chain tracking, recalls, shortages
3. **Evidence mapping** — Links entities to trials, efficacy, safety
4. **Indications & Contraindications** — Clinical decision support
5. **Drug-Drug Interactions** — Unlock clinical decision support with NDC corpus
6. **Biosimilar mappings** — Navigate regulatory paths between generics and biologics
